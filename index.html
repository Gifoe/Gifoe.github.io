Research

My research interests center on reasoning in large language models, large language models for scientific discovery, representation learning, and artificial intelligence for health. I am interested in how models can learn reusable representations and refine their reasoning through iterative computation, and how these capabilities can support scientific discovery and the analysis of real-world data.
