Research

My research interests center on reasoning in large language models (LLMs), large language models for science (LLM4S), representation learning, and artificial intelligence for health. I am interested in how models can learn reusable representations and improve their reasoning through iterative computation, and how these capabilities can support scientific discovery and the analysis of biomedical data.
